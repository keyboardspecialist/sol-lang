## Objective

Extend `SolMirRuntimeValues` with one same-ID static equality plan per recipe.
Plans describe recursive represented-value equality eligibility and traversal;
they do not execute equality or introduce runtime values, storage, allocation,
or a public executor API.

P3.2e does not add a production equality executor, failure sites, physical
runtime values, allocator, pointer encoding, copy or move/drop execution, host
transfer, cleanup lowering, interpreter work, MIR changes, or Wasm lowering.

## Approved Design

Equality-plan classifications are `UNREACHABLE`, `FORBIDDEN`, `TRIVIAL`,
`TEXT`, `PRODUCT`, `SUM`, and `WRAPPER`. Classification is derived recursively
from the authenticated recipes, layouts, allocation plans, and ownership plans,
independently of P2 `Copy` classification and independently of whether linkage
demands an equality import. `UNREACHABLE` remains distinct from `FORBIDDEN`.

- Primitive and initialized logical zero-sized leaves are `TRIVIAL` and compare
  by their represented primitive value without object access.
- `TEXT` compares lengths, then corresponding payload bytes in source order;
  zero-length text has no payload access.
- `PRODUCT` compares fields in increasing source ordinal, stopping at the first
  unequal child.
- `SUM` compares semantic tags first; unequal tags are `NOT_EQUAL`, and equal
  tags compare only the active variant's fields in increasing ordinal. Inactive
  payloads, padding, and representation bytes are ignored.
- A `WRAPPER` transparently delegates to its backing and has no second physical
  node or comparison.
- Callables, capabilities, and composites containing either are `FORBIDDEN`.
  They are not compared by identity, raw representation, tokens, targets, or
  captured/private backing state.

Recursive structural eligibility is a recipe property: it must not depend on
`Copy`, copy imports, equality imports, or runtime-operation demand. Plans must
not recursively expand recursive recipes or duplicate recipes, layouts, digests,
imports, variants, allocation plans, ownership edges, or copy plans.

Operands are finite, live, fully initialized, correctly represented exclusive
trees with the correct inhabited active sum variant. Each operand independently
has no holes, cycles, repeated allocation identities, or internal aliases; a
wrapper and its backing are one physical node. The two operands may alias each
other, including at their roots or within corresponding subtrees. Equality is
read-only and allocation-free. It completely validates both operands before any
comparison, so a malformed later subtree cannot be hidden by an earlier unequal
value.

The test-only finite-instance model returns `EQUAL`, `NOT_EQUAL`, `INVALID`, or
`LIMIT`. It applies bounds of depth 256, 1,048,576 nodes, and 4,000,000 work
steps; exact fit succeeds. Malformed shape, forbidden class, or failed complete
operand validation yields `INVALID`; exceeded depth, node, or work bounds yields
`LIMIT`. These are model outcomes only: P3.2e introduces no production outcome
API, failure records, failure sites, masks, or precedence rules. P3.3 owns
failure placement and precedence.

## Validation, Metering, And Rendering

Construction is transactional. Independent validation reconstructs every
equality plan without construction helpers and checks complete arena ranges and
all raw, cross-arena, and predecessor alias relationships before dereferencing.
Construction and validation use exact checked bytes, work, and scratch metering;
persistent-arena and validator-scratch fault selection remains deterministic.

Canonical buffered rendering declares `equality-plans=true` and
`equality-execution=false`, emits stable digest-keyed classifications, and does
not expose raw IDs, pointers, capacities, paths, allocator state, or operand
contents. Rendering a malformed owner writes zero bytes.
