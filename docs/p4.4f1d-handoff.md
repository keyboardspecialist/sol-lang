## Bounded behavior

The selected increment is exactly one direct pure type-owned refinement call of
the form `type T = refined Int64 where helper(self)`. The source predicate call
uses the exact owned plain `Int64` SELF input and a plain `Bool` VALUE result. The
existing non-refinement `requires` path is unchanged. Beneath this predicate call,
the existing pure ordinary scalar callee closure may include call chains and
recursive SCCs; each target has one owned plain `Int64` parameter and a plain
`Int64` or `Bool` result, and target-owned predicates remain call-free.

Still rejected are wrappers around the call, computed SELF arguments, `ensures`
calls, Bool/Unit signature widening, nested predicate calls or mixed image/predicate
cycles, refined or Text target interiors, PanicText, and the existing guard-call
case. Whole-target plain-image checks, including dead blocks, remain. The
refinement-context branch preserves exact source binding, type-plan, backing and
nominal-recipe joins and full indexed refinement-body plus `CHECK_REFINED`
preflight. The NULL-index failure-preflight caller gains no fallback scan, cache,
or authentication bypass. No emitter, ABI, header/schema, IR, observer, or
allocation-interface change is part of this increment.

available to SELF during evaluation. Success alone clears backing initialization and

preserves the accepted f1b-to-f1c anchors: four early anchors stay unchanged and

counts, same-pin line-inclusive equality, and raw hashes remain strict; no line is
ignored or masked. The accepted D96/T203/Q129/R297 inventory digests are retained;
no observed-output golden was refreshed.

The nonfault-safety review closed NF1-R1/NF2-R1. Actual-owner exit checks the full
snapshot, including root/header, requested extent, and slack, then applies the
byte-first fence and latches the real sticky `preservation_failed` state before any
later owner read or free, including catalog, operand/accessor, and final-witness
false paths.
The eight hostile-owner cases mutate only the selected saved scalar in an
authenticated owner. A frozen-address expected-delta byte fence must succeed before
computing the mutation seal or traversing validators/backend consumers; the post-seal
fence also remains unconditional. Seal semantics and preservation eligibility are
separate: a semantic seal failure keeps the case failed and suppresses rejection
execution, but byte-safe restoration remains allowed. Restore only the saved scalar
and original authentication token, then require complete original-byte equality
before normal/final validators or owner destruction. No restoration reseal, cached
authentication, or unexpected-byte repair is used. A sticky preservation mismatch
blocks restoration, further owner use, and destruction; independent buffers may be
freed. Private checker controls do not damage a live owner; the eight actual mutation
cases separately exercise the real selected mutation and restoration. Canonical controls
retain caller-local typed block edges and result-PRED checks, namespace/root-order
independence across both owners, source/root/content definitions, full-byte and
