# P3.6 Implementation Handoff (Historical Design Snapshot)

This is the approved pre-implementation P3.6 design, captured against baseline
commit `4b146b5` (`harden runtime predecessor alias validation`). It is not a
progress report or a second queue: `TODO.md` remains the live and sole authority
for work status and execution order. At capture, P3.1--P3.5 were complete for the
frozen E6 profile and P3.6 was the next overall and production checkpoint.

## Objective and Boundary

Add a separate, bounded, backend-independent, address-stable
`SolMirRuntimeLoweredProgram` owner. It borrows authenticated immutable P3.1
runtime conventions, P3.2 runtime values, P3.3 cleanup policy, P3.4 host ABI,
and P3.5 handler ABI; every predecessor must outlive it. It is the single
validated, canonical runtime-lowered closure over the complete P2 executable
graph, not a replacement for any predecessor.

The owner joins only exact, authenticated, same-ID predecessor records. It
neither renumbers, copies, reinterprets, broadens, nor synthesizes predecessor
semantic records. P2 remains the source of executable concrete images,
predicates, operations, imports, recipes, provenance, types, layouts, symbols,
and linkage; P3.1 remains the source of runtime signatures/imports/failure
conventions; P3.2 values remain the source of allocation and value-operation
plans; P3.3 remains the source of cleanup policy; P3.4 remains the source of
host requirements and grants; and P3.5 remains the source of handler frames and
exits.

P3.6 freezes the complete static runtime-lowered program. It does **not** choose
or execute a physical ABI, lower or emit Wasm, provide an adapter, allocate or
execute runtime values, execute cleanup or handlers, create capability tokens,
add CLI/build support, or provide compiled execution. P4 owns backend execution
and the Wasm adapter; P5 owns `sol build` artifacts and differential execution.
This completes task 48B without completing 48C.

## Canonical Runtime-Lowered Tables

`SolMirRuntimeLoweredProgram` owns compact join tables, in authenticated P2
canonical order, covering every executable record in the complete P2 graph. A
table record preserves canonical predecessor identity; its local arena index,
address, capacity, pointer, path, source ordinal, and unstable dense ID are not
semantic and must not appear in rendering.

| Table | Required same-ID join and coverage |
| --- | --- |
| Image instructions and blocks | Every executable concrete image instruction and block, including complete CFG/edge/terminator relation, joins its concrete image, P2 operation references, P3.1 call/failure conventions, and applicable P3.3 cleanup transitions. |
| Predicate instructions and blocks | Every executable P2.6 predicate/import-contract body instruction and block joins its predicate body, semantic operations, P3.1 signature/import/failure records, and P3.3 policy exactly as demanded. |
| Semantic operation joins | Every executable source-independent P2 operation joins its exact P3.1 runtime operation/import/signature and, when demanded, exact P3.2 recipe/value plan, P3.3 cleanup/failure policy, P3.4 host record, or P3.5 frame. No source-expression evaluation or unresolved body envelope remains. |
| Signatures, imports, and recipes | Every used internal, host, indirect-table, predicate, and provider signature; every runtime import; and every demanded recipe operation/allocation/value plan is present once by its predecessor identity. |
| Cleanup and failure | Every executable cleanup event, transition, action, drop path, failure edge, and failure site joins the exact P3.3/P3.1 records, preserving inherited-versus-supplemental site provenance and first-failure policy. |
| Host requirements and grants | Every demanded P3.4 host requirement joins its exact sealed E3 profile and root-specific grant. Distinct grants and their requirement/grant incidence are retained; equal-looking roots, requirements, and grants may not collapse. |
| Handler frames and exits | Every reachable P3.5 frame, P2 enter/exit marker, and P3.3 `EXIT_HANDLER` action joins its exact frame/exit relation, lexical parent, provider operation/signature, root, and closed effect. |

The owner must explicitly distinguish **erased/not-demanded** from **missing**.
Loop proof obligations and other P2 records already designated runtime-erased are
not executable demands and therefore have no lowered-table row. An optional
runtime facility with a canonical predecessor record but no demand is represented
as not-demanded, never as a fabricated record. By contrast, an executable P2
record, demanded operation/import/recipe, cleanup/failure relation, host
requirement/grant incidence, or handler relation with no required same-ID join is
malformed and must reject construction/validation transactionally. `NONE` and
empty tables are valid only where the authenticated predecessor closure proves no
demand.

## Closure, Authentication, and Validation

Construction is transactional into an empty owner. It first authenticates the
complete predecessor chain and then derives the tables by bounded scans; it must
cover every executable P2 image and predicate graph record, including all
cross-owner joins. It must not accept a partial root, selected call subset, or
fixture-only closure as a complete program.

A separate validator reconstructs the complete lowered closure without calling
construction helpers or trusting derived join tables. Before typed traversal it
checks predecessor identity/lifetime chains, raw ranges, capacities, complete
arena consumption, same-ID coverage, exact table cardinalities, and all direct
and transitive aliases. Only after prerequisite authentication may it traverse
typed records; it must then prove complete transitive anti-aliasing across the
runtime-lowered owner and every borrowed P3.1--P3.5/P2 arena. Raw, forged,
cross-arena, stale, partial, duplicate, substituted, or transitive predecessor
aliases are invalid.

Independent validation must prove, at minimum:

- every executable image and predicate instruction/block is represented once and
  all CFG, semantic-operation, call/import/signature, cleanup/failure, host, and
  handler joins are exact;
- every P3.1 signature/import/failure-site, P3.2 recipe plan, P3.3 event/
  transition/action, P3.4 requirement/distinct-grant incidence, and P3.5 frame/
  exit used by P2 is joined once, with no unaccounted demand;
- no erased/not-demanded relation is treated as missing, and no required relation
  is silently erased or represented as not-demanded; and
- backend-independent types, layouts, identities, symbols, linkage, and source
  provenance remain authenticated predecessor facts rather than newly selected
  ABI facts.

Build and validation meter exact counts, owned bytes, scratch bytes, and work for
every table and total. Checked arithmetic, limits, persistent-owner and
validator-scratch fault injection, deterministic failure outcomes, and clean
rollback are required. A successful rebuild of the same authenticated input must
produce the same tables, resource usage, and rendering.

## Rendering

Rendering validates first, buffers the entire canonical versionless form, then
performs one caller-visible write. It renders the complete table closure and
canonical predecessor identities/relationships, including explicit erased and
not-demanded states, and declares runtime lowering as static/backend-independent.
It must not render local dense IDs, pointers, addresses, capacities, paths,
private-source data, roots/tokens, runtime values, physical ABI locations, Wasm
indices, adapter details, or live execution state. Any malformed owner produces
zero caller-visible bytes.

## Conformance and Malformed-Program Matrix

Add table-driven conformance and malformed-program tests over the complete P2
graph, not merely owner-local records. The matrix must include:

- complete E6 all-roots closure: images, predicates, semantic operations,
  signatures/imports/recipes, cleanup/failure, host requirement/grant incidence,
  handler absence where authenticated, exact resources, determinism, and
  canonical buffered rendering;
- `p35` and `p35_reordered` handler fixtures: same-ID frame/marker/cleanup-exit
  joins, lexical parents, exact root/effect/provider/signature relations, and
  deterministic output independent of source ordering where predecessors define
  canonical order;
- supplemental focused fixtures for direct/internal/indirect and host calls,
  recipe create/copy/drop/equal demand and non-demand, allocation plans,
  predicate and import-contract blocks, normal/failure cleanup actions and
  sites, root-specific host grants, and handler entry/exit paths;
- absent-but-erased and absent-but-not-demanded cases, separately from required
  joins that are missing; and
- malformed predecessor chains; wrong IDs; partial or duplicate coverage;
  substituted signatures/imports/recipes/operations; forged or cross-owner
  aliases (including transitive aliases); broken CFG/instruction/block joins;
  failure/cleanup mismatch; collapsed distinct grants or bad incidence; handler
  frame/exit mismatch; limits/overflow/fault rollback; deterministic rebuild;
  and malformed zero-write rendering.

Inherited E6 and focused example counts from planning are **provisional until
the implementation census**. Freeze only implementation-derived counts after
the complete graph, resource meters, and conformance matrix are accepted; do not
backfill or alter predecessor censuses.

## Historical Addendum: Canonical Output and Exact Resources

Canonical rendering invariance applies to the rendered runtime-lowered closure,
not to its resource accounting. Borrowed authenticated text, including source
paths and names, is inspected byte-by-byte by exact validation and its lengths
therefore contribute to `validation_work`. Equivalent canonical renderings may
have different exact validation-work totals when their borrowed text lengths
differ; each authenticated fixture freezes and validates its own total.

## Final Slice E Census Addendum

Slice E validates before allocating its private render buffers and makes one
caller-visible write only after its complete canonical relation inventory is
assembled.  It declares static, backend-independent lowering and writes a
present/not-demanded/erased census followed by one typed-key relationship line
for every owned row, in `SOL_MIR_RUNTIME_LOWERED_TABLES` order.  Rows sort by
their complete typed key and relationship; ordered child relationships retain
their semantic occurrence order.  It does not hash raw row bytes, padding, or
arena coordinates.  Linkage instance, recipe, host requirement, table, and
import identities use their authenticated 256-bit keys; image and predicate
relations use authenticated parent keys plus relative B/N/E coordinates.
Variable ordered children are serialized as explicit counts plus
length-framed, domain-separated semantic digests; no renderer child list has a
fixed serialization buffer.  The 4096-byte staged-line bound covers only fixed
fields and is included exactly in the sealed render scratch bound.

`max_render_bytes` and `max_render_scratch_bytes` bound the serialized upper
bound and sort staging separately from owner/build resources.  Their measured
upper bounds are sealed and independently validated.  A malformed owner,
render-limit failure, overflow, or render-allocation fault emits zero
caller-visible bytes.  Header and census lines contain semantic inventory only,
not resource-usage meters.
