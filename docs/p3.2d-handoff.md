## Objective

Extend `SolMirRuntimeValues` with one same-ID static
`SolMirRuntimeCopyPlan` per recipe, plus a copy-plan arena and its limits and
usage. Plans describe deep-copy eligibility and traversal only; they do not
execute a copy or introduce a runtime value, storage, allocator, or public
execution API.

P3.2d does not add a production runtime value, physical allocator, pointer
encoding, copy executor, interpreter work, MIR changes, P2/P3.1 changes,
equality execution, host transfer, cleanup lowering, or Wasm lowering.

## Approved Design

Copy-plan classifications are `UNREACHABLE`, `FORBIDDEN`, `TRIVIAL`, `TEXT`,
`PRODUCT`, `SUM`, and `WRAPPER`. Classification derives independently from P2
`is_copy`/`copy_kind` and the P3.2c ownership classification. `FORBIDDEN`
includes callables, capabilities, and non-Copy composites; `UNREACHABLE` is a
distinct classification. Trivial leaves require neither imports nor allocation.

Copy plans must not duplicate layouts, digests, imports, variants, or ownership
edges. Existing authenticated recipe, layout, allocation-plan, ownership-plan,
variant, and edge data remains authoritative.

The abstract whole-source precondition is a live, fully initialized, available,
finite, exclusive tree with the correct inhabited active sum variant. It has no
holes, aliases, cycles, or repeated allocation identities. The destination is
distinct, empty, and unpublished. Inactive sum payload, padding, and other
inactive representation bytes are ignored. A wrapper and its backing are one
physical node.

The prescribed traversal is depth-first source order:

- A product or sum allocates its outer object before its active children, which
  are visited in increasing ordinal order. A sum copies tag metadata only.
- Text allocates/copies its header before a nonzero payload.
- A wrapper delegates to its backing and performs no second allocation.
- Initialized logical zero-sized values are copied despite requiring no object.
- Callables and capabilities are forbidden. Owned handles are never shallow
  copied.

Complete preflight precedes every allocation. It validates a finite instance
traversal and uses ceilings of depth 256, 1,048,576 nodes, and 4,000,000 work
steps (the test model may use tighter bounds). It performs checked scratch and
identity handling, target/object/length/arithmetic checks, and aggregate quota
demand using the P3.2b plans and checker. Exact fit succeeds. A preflight
failure makes no allocation, mutation, or quota charge.

After preflight, stage privately in traversal order. Charge every successful
allocation immediately; a refused request is uncharged. Rollback never refunds
successful cumulative quota charges. Publish the destination exactly once, only
after all fallible work succeeds. On failure the destination remains unavailable
and the source is unchanged. Rollback uses the P3.2c initialized-subtree
postorder, not reverse allocation order; it is non-fallible and must not leak or
double free.

Existing `ALLOCATION_LIMIT` and `ALLOCATION_FAILED` outcomes apply abstractly.
Invalid shape or aliasing is invalid input. Depth, node, and work ceilings are
modeled value/step limits, but P3.2d adds no production failure records, sites,
or masks. P3.3 owns placement and failure precedence.

## Validation, Metering, And Rendering

Construction is transactional and validation independently reconstructs every
copy plan. Both use exact checked bytes, work, and scratch metering. Validation
checks complete ranges and all cross-arena and predecessor alias relationships
before dereferencing. Persistent-arena and validator-scratch fault selection
must remain deterministic. Rendering a malformed owner writes zero bytes.

Canonical buffered rendering declares `copy-plans=true` and
`copy-execution=false`, and emits a stable copy classification. It must not
expose raw IDs, pointers, capacities, paths, or allocator state.
